Bibi School Reaches Grade 8

After 5 years of growth, Bibi Female School has advanced to Grade 8, serving 400+ students. We continue working toward 12th grade to prepare students for university entry exams.

March 2026
English & Computer Program Expansion

85 girls above age 16 are now enrolled in our intensive English and Computer Science program. To date, 25 students have graduated from all three levels.

Online Bachelor Degree Initiative Launched

HHRDO begins partnerships with international universities to facilitate online bachelor degrees for qualified Afghan students, removing geographic barriers to higher education.
