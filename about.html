About HHRDO

Our Story

From a family initiative in Norway to a registered NGO in Afghanistan, HHRDO is built on the conviction that education is the pathway to breaking cycles of poverty and conflict.

Supporting Education & Development in Afghanistan

Our Foundation

From Norway to Afghanistan

HHRDO was established in 2018 as a family-funded initiative by members of the Wolasmal family residing in Norway. What started as a grassroots effort gradually attracted a growing network of friends and supporters who shared our vision.

Witnessing exponential growth and mounting demand for our services, we formalized our activities into a structured organization. In January 2023, HHRDO was officially registered with Afghan authorities as an NGO, marking our commitment to sustainable, legitimate operations.

Our Founding Values

Family-driven commitment to education • Community-centered approach • Sustainable growth • Transparency and accountability

Our Mission

Fighting Illiteracy, Building Futures

Illiteracy and ignorance are known to be the main causes of war, conflicts, and poverty in Afghanistan. Our mission is to address this fundamental issue by providing and facilitating education opportunities for deprived children in rural communities.

We focus on four primary areas:

  • Education: Provide quality schooling combining modern education with religious studies
  • Capacity Building: Develop infrastructure, facilities, and teacher training programs
  • Welfare Support: Assist orphans and widows through monthly grants and aid
  • Community Development: Build sustainable ecosystems for long-term growth

Our Approach

A Balanced Model

Our educational model combines modern educational principles with religious studies—a deliberate strategy with important benefits:

  • Minimizes resistance from parents in conservative religious communities
  • Avoids conflicts with religious groups and political factions
  • Maintains community trust and stability
  • Aligns with local values while providing contemporary education

Our policy of neutrality has proven effective: Despite the Taliban's transition to power in 2021 and subsequent restrictions on girls' education, our schools and activities have remained largely untouched—a testament to our commitment to community partnership and culturally sensitive operations.

What We Teach

Comprehensive Curriculum

Our curriculum provides students with a well-rounded education preparing them for university, employment, and responsible citizenship:

  • Mathematics & Numeracy
  • English Language Skills
  • Reading & Writing
  • Pashto Language & Literature
  • Health & Social Education
  • Religious Studies & Islamic Values
  • Computer Literacy & Digital Skills

We believe a comprehensive education unlocks human potential and creates pathways out of poverty.
