Our Approach

Breaking the Cycle of Poverty

In Afghanistan, the loss of a breadwinner due to conflict, accident, or illness often forces families into survival mode. Orphaned children and widows face extreme poverty, pushing children into child labor instead of school.

HHRDO's welfare program directly addresses this crisis by providing monthly grants to 73 vulnerable families. Each widow or orphan household receives approximately $25 per month—enough to cover basic needs and allow children to attend school.

Monthly Grant Impact

$25/month × 73 families = $1,825 monthly

= $21,900 annually

The Difference

Changing Trajectories

Without HHRDO Support: Children work instead of study, perpetuating illiteracy and poverty across generations. Families remain trapped in survival mode with no path forward.

With HHRDO Support: Children attend school and receive education. Families maintain dignity and stability. Students develop skills and knowledge to build better futures.

This welfare program is education in action—by removing the economic pressure that forces children to work, we unlock their potential to learn, grow, and eventually lift their families out of poverty.
